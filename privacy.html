Privacy & responsible use

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026 · Public prototype and Android beta

RUMOURPRINT AI is an investigative research aid, not a source of final factual verdicts. Do not enter confidential reporting materials, private source identities, personal contact details, or other sensitive information.

What the tool does

RUMOURPRINT AI offers one simple investigation form with news search, public social post search and image research. Searches return source links, not a complete index or a verdict. Results are investigative leads requiring independent editorial verification.

Information you choose to submit

The app processes the public claim, where you encountered it, and any picture or review text you choose to submit. Search results are kept in a temporary app session and may disappear when it expires. There is no visitor account or guaranteed permanent case storage. A separately configured moderated community-review database may store voluntary notes for later visitors. Hosting and external providers may maintain operational or security logs under their own policies.

Searching public stories and checking pictures

When you tap Investigate, the application generates search terms from the public claim and searches Google News, plus additional web and fact-check services when the administrator enables them. These services receive the search terms. RUMOURPRINT displays discovered links but does not copy publisher websites, determine a claim's truth, or guarantee that every article is indexed.

When you upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP photo for provenance review, the bytes are processed by the Streamlit-hosted server to inspect image metadata and C2PA Content Credentials, where available. Image text and file information are examined on the app server. If you explicitly enable internet picture matching and FEM has configured the provider, the image is sent to Google Cloud Vision to search for similar images on public pages. Avoid private photographs and images of vulnerable people. C2PA data may be absent or stripped, and no percentage of AI-altered pixels can be reliably calculated.

Finding public social posts

When you tap Investigate, the app can search public social post links through an enabled web-search provider. With X API enabled by FEM, the application also sends search keywords to X to retrieve limited, recent public posts and their original links. Other platforms may be searchable through public indexed links or searches opened manually. These searches are incomplete; they cannot access private posts or guarantee that a deleted post will be found.

Suggestions for possible institutional X accounts are not proof of real-world account ownership, and a blue badge, screenshot or copied quote cannot prove who wrote a statement. Confirm the profile using an independently controlled institutional source and inspect the original post. Do not submit private messages, source identities, passwords or personal information.

External search providers

The app searches Google News using the keywords it generates. When configured, it can also send those terms to Brave Search and Google Fact Check Tools API; users are not asked for provider keys. Opening links leaves RUMOURPRINT for the original site, which has its own privacy practices. Remove private names or confidential details before submitting a claim.

Hosting and browser data

The FEM Tools landing page is delivered through Cloudflare Pages, and the investigation workspace is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. These services may use operational logs, browser storage, cookies, or security controls. This page does not claim their data is never retained. If website analytics are enabled, their operation is governed by the relevant provider settings and policies.

Android beta installer

The Android app is currently a debug-signed beta distributed through a GitHub release for testing. It uses internet for live investigations and has an offline library of original educational articles. The offline library is not an offline AI fact-checker. It is not a Play Store release. Future release-signed builds may not install over this beta.

Editorial limitations

Similarity scores do not determine whether a claim is true or false, which message came first, who wrote it, how widely it spread, or whether accounts are coordinated. Luganda support remains experimental. Users remain responsible for reviewing original documents, publication dates and source credibility.

Offline library and visitor notes

The offline library contains 20 original educational guides, and your browser may save them for reading without a connection. Stories you personally save are stored on your device and can disappear if its browser data is cleared. The Android beta bundles these guides; live investigations still need internet access. External publisher content is not copied in full without rights.

If the community review service is enabled, voluntarily submitted notes are held for FEM moderation before becoming visible to other visitors. If the service is not configured, the app offers a personal note download instead. Only share public information and open source links before acting on a reader's advice.

Questions or feedback

For organisational enquiries, use the contact channels published on the Fresh Exclusive Media website. Avoid sending sensitive case materials through public channels.
