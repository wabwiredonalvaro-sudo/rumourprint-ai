Privacy & responsible use

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026 · Public prototype and Android beta

RUMOURPRINT AI is an investigative research aid, not a source of final factual verdicts. Do not enter confidential reporting materials, private source identities, personal contact details, or other sensitive information.

What the tool does

RUMOURPRINT AI offers one simple investigation form with news search, public social post search and image research. Searches return source links, not a complete index or a verdict. Results are investigative leads requiring independent editorial verification.

Information you choose to submit

The application processes the claims, transcripts, optional source labels, search terms and notes that you enter. It currently offers no user accounts or dedicated permanent case database. Your working claim library is maintained in the app session; sessions can reset or expire, and the project cannot guarantee recovery of submitted work. Export important reports yourself. Hosting and service providers may maintain technical or security logs subject to their own policies.

Publisher article extraction and image review

When you request article extraction, the app server downloads an HTML article from a supported publisher and extracts selected text passages for a preliminary source comparison. External reporting can be outdated, misleading or quoted out of context; a human reviewer must verify the citation and decision. Not all sites allow automated extraction.

When you upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP photo for provenance review, the bytes are processed by the Streamlit-hosted server to inspect image metadata and C2PA Content Credentials, where available. Images are not deliberately sent to third-party AI detection APIs by this feature. Avoid private photographs and images of vulnerable people. C2PA data may be absent or stripped, and no percentage of AI-altered pixels can be reliably calculated.

Checking public X accounts

The Official Social Accounts feature lets a journalist specify a public X @handle, an alleged quotation, and (optionally) a direct X post URL. In manual mode, a search link opens on X in the user's browser. If a newsroom administrator enables X API access, account handles, search terms and post IDs are sent to X's official API for targeted public-post lookups. X may charge the project for results retrieved; the application therefore does not make background lookups or harvest private accounts.

Only short excerpts, post URLs and account IDs are presented in ephemeral app sessions and downloaded research briefs. An X handle, badge, or apparent wording match is not proof of the real-world author's identity. The journalist must verify the person-account relationship independently and examine the original post. Do not submit private or sensitive statements, source identities, passwords or personal messages.

External evidence searches

When you choose to search published evidence, your edited query is sent from the application to Google News search. If you enable published fact-check search and supply your own Google Fact Check Tools API key, the query and key are sent to that Google service. The separate external sites you open from search results have their own privacy practices. Review search terms carefully and remove personal or identifying details before submitting them.

Hosting and browser data

The FEM Tools landing page is delivered through Cloudflare Pages, and the investigation workspace is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. These services may use operational logs, browser storage, cookies, or security controls. This page does not claim their data is never retained. If website analytics are enabled, their operation is governed by the relevant provider settings and policies.

Android beta installer

The Android app is currently a debug-signed beta distributed through a GitHub release for testing. It uses internet for live investigations and has an offline library of original educational articles. The offline library is not an offline AI fact-checker. It is not a Play Store release. Future release-signed builds may not install over this beta.

Editorial limitations

Similarity scores do not determine whether a claim is true or false, which message came first, who wrote it, how widely it spread, or whether accounts are coordinated. Luganda support remains experimental. Users remain responsible for reviewing original documents, publication dates and source credibility.

Offline library and visitor notes

The offline library contains 20 original educational guides, and your browser may save them for reading without a connection. Stories you personally save are stored on your device and can disappear if its browser data is cleared. The Android beta bundles these guides; live investigations still need internet access. External publisher content is not copied in full without rights.

If the community review service is enabled, voluntarily submitted notes are held for FEM moderation before becoming visible to other visitors. If the service is not configured, the app offers a personal note download instead. Only share public information and open source links before acting on a reader's advice.

Questions or feedback

For organisational enquiries, use the contact channels published on the Fresh Exclusive Media website. Avoid sending sensitive case materials through public channels.
