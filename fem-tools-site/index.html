Free public research prototype · Android 8.0+ · Requires internet · No account needed. Never enter confidential newsroom information.

Built for the investigative workflow

RUMOURPRINT AI surfaces similarities and possible changes in wording. It does not independently establish the truth of a claim.

01 / INVESTIGATE

Compare related claims

Identify messages discussing the same topic despite changed wording, then examine where meaning or certainty may have shifted.

02 / VISUALISE

Map narrative connections

View similarity links and connected message groups in an interactive relationship map for further journalistic review.

03 / RESEARCH

Look for published evidence

Explore related news headlines and available fact-check reviews, follow original sources and document findings.

A tool for journalists, not a truth machine.

Similarity alone cannot determine accuracy, source attribution or how a message spread. Verify claims against original documents, dates and trusted reporting before publishing conclusions.
