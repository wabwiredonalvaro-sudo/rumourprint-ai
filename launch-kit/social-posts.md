## Suggested replies to comments
**Does it automatically detect fake news?** No. It highlights similarities and changes in claims, then points you to evidence for independent verification.

**Is it free?** The current public research prototype is accessible online without a user account.

**Can it work without internet?** The public website and Android beta both require internet access.

**Can I save investigations?** You can export reports, but the current prototype does not offer permanent user accounts or guaranteed case storage.
