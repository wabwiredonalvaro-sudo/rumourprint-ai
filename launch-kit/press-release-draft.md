## RUMOURPRINT AI opens public web access to narrative investigation tools

**Draft announcement | Kampala, Uganda | For publication after final editorial approval**

Fresh Exclusive Media is opening public access to **RUMOURPRINT AI**, a browser-based investigation workspace designed to help journalists and members of the public examine how suspicious messages change as they are repeated.

Available through **https://tools.freshexclusivemedia.com/**, the platform lets users submit public claims, compare potentially related messages, examine changes in wording and certainty, explore narrative relationship maps, and follow links to published news and fact-check research.

RUMOURPRINT AI uses a Python-based multilingual semantic matching model alongside transparent comparison rules. It identifies possible relationships and editorial review cues; it **does not declare whether a claim is true or false**. Reporters must still inspect primary documents, dates and context.

An Android beta is also available from the same FEM Tools portal. The beta is a test build requiring internet access and is not yet distributed through Google Play.

**Access:** https://tools.freshexclusivemedia.com/  
**Launch web tool:** https://rumourprint-fem.streamlit.app/  
**Android beta:** https://github.com/wabwiredonalvaro-sudo/rumourprint-ai/releases/tag/android-v0.1.0-beta.1

**About Fresh Exclusive Media:** Fresh Exclusive Media is the developer and publisher of RUMOURPRINT AI as part of its digital journalism and media innovation work.

**Important editorial note:** The public prototype uses temporary session data rather than permanent accounts. Users should avoid submitting confidential sources or private information.
